
# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in this project.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive behavior include:
- Being respectful and considerate.
- Welcoming constructive feedback.
- Accepting different viewpoints.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, insults, and sharing private information without permission.

## Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions when representing the project.

## Enforcement
Unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Appropriate corrective action may be taken.

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the Contributor Covenant.
